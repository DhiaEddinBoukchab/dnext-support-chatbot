# Dnext Business Page Snapshot
Company Overview

DNEXT Intelligence SA is a privately-owned and independent Swiss-based company
specializing in agriculture commodity expertise.

The company positions itself as a provider of commodities market analysis and
agricultural market research, with coverage across the global agricultural
landscape.

Its public business message focuses on helping agri-traders and agribusiness
professionals use data insights to improve strategy, price forecasting, and
decision-making.


Core Value Proposition

DNEXT presents its offering as data-driven support for agricultural trade.

The public website message highlights these outcomes:

- identify market opportunities
- better understand market trends
- improve risk management
- improve decision-making
- support pricing decisions


Solutions And Target Industries

The public website states that DNEXT solutions are used by several industry
segments, including:

- biofuel producers
- commodity trading houses
- farmers
- financial institutions
- food manufacturers

Products Overview

The public website highlights these product areas:

- Market Intelligence Platform
- TradeFlow
- Weather

The platform is described as an agriculture data intelligence offering for
agribusiness professionals who need planning, market visibility, and future
oriented decision support.

TradeFlow is described as a product for understanding how agricultural products
are traded and consumed around the world.

Weather is described as a product that helps agricultural professionals manage
crop-cycle decisions, market volatility, and operational risk through weather
data insights.



Brand Messaging

The homepage uses the business message:

Harvesting Data, Growing Excellence

It emphasizes empowering agricultural market participants with advanced data
insights to elevate strategies, forecast prices, and stay ahead in the industry.
